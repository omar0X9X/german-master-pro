# Roadmap
## Foundation
Next.js, TypeScript, adaptive engine, FSRS, mastery/unlock, AI abstraction, Supabase/RLS.
## Product
Complete learner routes, placement, content APIs, speaking/pronunciation UX, mistake-bank persistence, exam flows.
## Content scale
Grow curated bilingual datasets toward the specification targets: 5k vocabulary items, 300 grammar concepts, 100 reading texts and 200 listening tracks. Generated content must pass human/automated validation before production.
## Reliability
CI, E2E, accessibility audits, rate limits, observability, backups and release automation.
